The great sofa debate draws local interest

August 22, 2026 · Jasper’s notebook

At a recent household meeting, Jasper presented his position on sofa access by occupying most of the sofa. The demonstration was persuasive, although it left very little room for the other participants.

His proposal includes one cushion for his head, one for his paws, and an unspecified number of cushions for future expansion. A human suggested the dog bed instead. Jasper requested that the suggestion be entered into the record and quietly ignored.

A compromise was eventually reached: Jasper would move over a little, and everybody would pretend that this was enough.
