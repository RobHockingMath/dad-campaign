Thank you for all the belly rubs!

August 5, 2026 · Jasper’s notebook

Jasper would like to thank everyone who stopped to say hello this summer. Each friendly greeting, patient ear scratch, and carefully negotiated belly rub has been received with considerable enthusiasm.

He had planned to write individual thank-you notes, but the pen was difficult to hold and the envelopes were surprisingly interesting to chew.

Please accept a slow tail wag and a gentle lean against your leg in place of a handwritten card.
