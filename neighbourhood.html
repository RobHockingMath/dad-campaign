What is Jasper’s neighbourhood?

Jasper’s neighbourhood is the collection of paths, gardens, doorsteps, and familiar faces that make up his daily world. Its boundaries are informal and are occasionally extended when somebody suggests a longer walk.

The area includes several places of particular importance:

  • The front gate, where every outing begins with great optimism.
  • The shady path, which offers an excellent selection of smells.
  • The little patch of grass where Jasper likes to watch the world go by.
  • The kitchen, a centre of considerable economic activity.
  • The sofa, currently the subject of an ongoing space-sharing agreement.

What connects these places is the company along the way. Jasper is in favour of a neighbourhood where people have time to stop, dogs have room to walk, and nobody feels left out.
